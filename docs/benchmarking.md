# Feature Benchmarking — Second-Hand Marketplace Platforms

## 1. Introduction

This document presents a feature benchmarking of five major second-hand marketplace platforms: **OLX**, **Vinted**, **eBay**, **Wallapop**, and **Facebook Marketplace**. The goal is to map the feature landscape these competitors offer, identify common patterns, and spot gaps and opportunities that can inform Trevo's product decisions.

Each platform is analysed across a consistent set of dimensions: core features, user management, communication, transactions & payments, monetisation, unique/differentiating features, safety & trust, and social features.

---

## 2. Platform-by-Platform Analysis

### 2.1 OLX

#### Core Features
- **Listings:** Free ad creation with title, description, price, condition, and location.
- **Categories:** Broad category tree (electronics, vehicles, real estate, fashion, home & garden, jobs, services, etc.).
- **Search & Filters:** Keyword search with filters for category, price range, condition (new/used), location radius, and sort order (date, price).
- **Images:** Multiple photos per listing (typically up to 8). No mandatory image.

#### User Management
- **Registration:** Email, phone number, or social login (Google, Facebook).
- **Profiles:** Basic public profile showing active listings, join date, and response time.
- **Verification:** Phone number verification; optional ID verification in some markets.
- **Reputation:** No formal rating system in most markets; some regions have a basic review feature.

#### Communication
- **Messaging:** In-app chat between buyer and seller. Supports text and images.
- **Notifications:** Push notifications and email alerts for messages, price drops, and saved-search matches.

#### Transactions & Payments
- **Methods:** Primarily cash on meetup. Some markets offer OLX Pay (integrated card/bank payments with escrow).
- **Shipping:** OLX Delivery available in select markets (integrated label generation and tracking).
- **Buyer/Seller Protection:** OLX Pay includes buyer protection (refund if item not as described); otherwise, no protection for cash deals.

#### Monetisation
- **Fees:** Listing is free. OLX Pay charges a small transaction fee.
- **Promoted Listings:** Paid "featured" and "top ad" placements to boost visibility.
- **Subscriptions:** Business/dealer packages with bulk listing tools and analytics.

#### Unique / Differentiating Features
- Strong focus on local, in-person transactions.
- Lightweight listing flow designed for speed.

#### Safety & Trust
- **Reporting:** Users can report listings or other users for fraud, prohibited items, or spam.
- **Moderation:** Automated filters for prohibited content; manual review teams.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Safety tips displayed in chat; OLX Pay escrow reduces fraud for shipped items.

#### Social Features
- **Favourites:** Users can save/favourite listings for later.
- **Sharing:** Listings can be shared via link, WhatsApp, or social media.
- **Following:** No follow mechanism for users or shops.

### 2.2 Vinted

#### Core Features
- **Listings:** Item listing with title, description, brand, size, condition, colour, price, and category.
- **Categories:** Fashion-centric categories (women, men, kids clothing, shoes, bags, accessories, beauty, home).
- **Search & Filters:** Advanced keyword search with filters for brand, size, condition, colour, price range, material, and sort order.
- **Images:** Up to 20 photos per listing. At least 1 image required.

#### User Management
- **Registration:** Email, Google, Facebook, or Apple ID.
- **Profiles:** Public profile with photo, bio, ratings, number of sales, verification badges, last active time, and wardrobe (all listings).
- **Verification:** Email, phone, and optional government ID verification.
- **Reputation:** Star rating system (1–5) with written reviews after each completed transaction. Ratings are public.

#### Communication
- **Messaging:** In-app messaging with text and image support. Includes an "offer" button to negotiate price directly in chat.
- **Notifications:** Push, email, and in-app notifications for messages, offers, order updates, price drops, and saved-search matches.

#### Transactions & Payments
- **Methods:** Integrated payments via credit/debit card, bank transfer, or Vinted Wallet balance.
- **Shipping:** Built-in shipping with pre-paid labels; multiple carrier options; package tracking included.
- **Buyer/Seller Protection:** Vinted Buyer Protection on every transaction — refund if item not received or significantly not as described. Funds held in escrow until buyer confirms receipt.

#### Monetisation
- **Fees:** No listing fee and no seller commission. A buyer protection fee (typically ~5% + fixed amount) is charged to the buyer.
- **Promoted Listings:** "Item Bump" paid feature to increase visibility in search results.
- **Subscriptions:** No subscription tiers.

#### Unique / Differentiating Features
- Zero seller fees — sellers keep 100% of the sale price.
- Fashion-focused niche with size, brand, and material filters tailored to clothing.
- Vinted Wallet for quick payouts and re-spending within the platform.

#### Safety & Trust
- **Reporting:** Report listings, users, or messages for policy violations.
- **Moderation:** Automated content moderation (image recognition, text filters) plus human review.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Escrow payments; seller identity verification; suspicious-activity detection algorithms.

#### Social Features
- **Favourites:** Heart/save listings; sellers are notified when someone favourites their item.
- **Sharing:** Share listings via link or social media.
- **Following:** Users can follow other members' wardrobes; activity feed shows new listings from followed users.

### 2.3 eBay

#### Core Features
- **Listings:** Detailed listings with title, description, item specifics (brand, model, condition, dimensions), price, and quantity.
- **Categories:** Extremely broad category taxonomy covering virtually every product type — electronics, fashion, collectibles, vehicles, home, sports, business & industrial, etc.
- **Search & Filters:** Powerful search with filters for category, price, condition (new/used/refurbished), location, seller rating, free shipping, and many item-specific attributes.
- **Images:** Up to 24 photos per listing. Gallery view and zoom.

#### User Management
- **Registration:** Email or Google/Apple/Facebook.
- **Profiles:** Detailed profile with feedback score, positive feedback percentage, member-since date, and recent feedback comments.
- **Verification:** Email and payment method verification. PayPal or bank account linkage. Business seller verification.
- **Reputation:** Detailed feedback system — buyers and sellers rate each transaction as positive/neutral/negative with comments. Feedback score accumulates over time. Detailed Seller Ratings (DSR) on item description accuracy, communication, shipping time, and shipping cost.

#### Communication
- **Messaging:** eBay messaging system for buyer-seller communication. Structured messaging with order-related templates.
- **Notifications:** Email, push, and in-app notifications for order updates, messages, watched-item changes, and saved-search matches.

#### Transactions & Payments
- **Methods:** eBay Managed Payments (credit/debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfer).
- **Shipping:** Seller-arranged or eBay-integrated shipping labels (discounted USPS/UPS/FedEx rates). Global Shipping Programme for international sales. Tracking and estimated delivery dates.
- **Buyer/Seller Protection:** eBay Money Back Guarantee — full refund if item not received or not as described. Seller protections against fraudulent claims.

#### Monetisation
- **Fees:** Insertion fee (first ~250 listings/month free, then ~$0.35/listing). Final value fee (~13% of sale price including shipping). Payment processing fee included.
- **Promoted Listings:** Promoted Listings Standard (pay a percentage on sale), Promoted Listings Advanced (cost-per-click), and display ads.
- **Subscriptions:** eBay Store subscriptions (Starter, Basic, Premium, Anchor, Enterprise) with reduced fees, more free listings, and marketing tools.

#### Unique / Differentiating Features
- "Best Offer" — buyers can negotiate on fixed-price listings.
- Authenticity Guarantee for luxury items (sneakers, watches, handbags) — items verified by independent authenticators.
- Refurbished programme with manufacturer or eBay-certified refurbished items and warranties.
- Massive global reach and cross-border trade support.

#### Safety & Trust
- **Reporting:** Report listings, sellers, or messages for policy violations, counterfeits, or fraud.
- **Moderation:** VeRO programme (IP rights enforcement), automated listing removal for prohibited items, and human review.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Money Back Guarantee; seller performance standards enforcement; suspicious-activity monitoring; address verification.

#### Social Features
- **Favourites:** Watchlist for tracking listings.
- **Sharing:** Share listings via link, email, or social media.
- **Following:** Follow sellers to see new listings; saved sellers list.

### 2.4 Wallapop

#### Core Features
- **Listings:** Item listing with title, description, price, condition, category, and location.
- **Categories:** General categories — electronics, fashion, home, sports, vehicles, motorbikes, real estate, etc.
- **Search & Filters:** Keyword search with filters for category, price range, condition, distance/location, and sort order (relevance, price, date).
- **Images:** Up to 10 photos per listing.

#### User Management
- **Registration:** Email, Google, Facebook, or Apple ID.
- **Profiles:** Public profile with photo, join date, number of sales, average response time, and reviews.
- **Verification:** Email and phone verification. Optional ID verification for Wallapop Shipping.
- **Reputation:** Star ratings (1–5) and written reviews after completed transactions. "Super Seller" badge for consistent high performance.

#### Communication
- **Messaging:** In-app chat with text, images, and integrated price-offer functionality (buyer can propose a price with one tap).
- **Notifications:** Push and email notifications for messages, offers, order updates, and saved-search results.

#### Transactions & Payments
- **Methods:** Cash for local meetups. Wallapop Shipping integrates card payments via Wallapay.
- **Shipping:** Built-in shipping with pre-paid labels, multiple carrier options, and tracking for Wallapop Shipping transactions.
- **Buyer/Seller Protection:** Wallapop Shipping includes buyer protection (refund if item not received or not as described); funds held until buyer confirms. No protection for cash meetups.

#### Monetisation
- **Fees:** Free to list. Wallapop Shipping charges the buyer a protection fee. Seller pays no commission.
- **Promoted Listings:** "Highlight" and "Feature" paid options to boost listing visibility.
- **Subscriptions:** Pro accounts for frequent or professional sellers with analytics and bulk tools.

#### Unique / Differentiating Features
- One-tap offer button in listing view and chat — very low-friction negotiation.
- "Wallapop Cars" sub-platform with specialised vehicle listings including make, model, mileage, and year filters.
- "Super Seller" badge rewards reliable sellers.

#### Safety & Trust
- **Reporting:** Report users, listings, or chat messages.
- **Moderation:** Automated and manual moderation of listings and images.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Meeting-point suggestions for safe local exchanges; escrow through Wallapop Shipping; scam detection.

#### Social Features
- **Favourites:** Save listings and receive price-drop alerts.
- **Sharing:** Share listings via link, WhatsApp, or social media.
- **Following:** Follow sellers to receive notifications about their new listings.

### 2.5 Facebook Marketplace

#### Core Features
- **Listings:** Listing creation with title, price, description, condition, category, location, and product tags.
- **Categories:** Broad categories — vehicles, property rentals/sales, clothing, electronics, furniture, garden, toys, and more.
- **Search & Filters:** Keyword search with filters for category, price range, condition, distance/location, seller type (individual/business), and date listed.
- **Images:** Up to 10 photos per listing.

#### User Management
- **Registration:** Requires an existing Facebook account.
- **Profiles:** Marketplace profile is the user's Facebook profile — shows name, profile picture, mutual friends, and Marketplace ratings. Users can see the seller's general Facebook activity and friend count.
- **Verification:** Tied to Facebook's identity system (email, phone, and in some cases ID verification for Facebook identity).
- **Reputation:** Star ratings (1–5) and optional written reviews visible on the Marketplace profile. Rating is separate from the main Facebook profile.

#### Communication
- **Messaging:** Facebook Messenger is used for all buyer-seller communication — text, images, voice messages, video calls.
- **Notifications:** Standard Facebook/Messenger notifications for messages, price drops, saved-search results.

#### Transactions & Payments
- **Methods:** Primarily cash on meetup or external payment methods arranged by buyer and seller. In some markets, integrated checkout with credit/debit card and PayPal for shipped items.
- **Shipping:** Available in select markets (US primarily) — sellers can offer shipping with integrated labels and tracking.
- **Buyer/Seller Protection:** Purchase Protection available for checkout-enabled transactions (refund if item not received or not as described). No protection for local cash deals.

#### Monetisation
- **Fees:** Free for individual sellers. 5% selling fee (or $0.40 minimum) on shipped items using checkout. Businesses pay for shop features.
- **Promoted Listings:** Boost listings using Facebook Ads infrastructure — pay-per-impression or pay-per-click.
- **Subscriptions:** No subscription model for individuals. Commerce Manager for business sellers.

#### Unique / Differentiating Features
- Embedded within Facebook's ecosystem — massive built-in user base with zero separate registration required.
- Social graph integration — buyers can see mutual friends with sellers, adding a layer of social trust.
- Local community groups — integration with Facebook Buy & Sell groups extends reach.
- AI-powered personalised feed based on Facebook's interest and behaviour data.

#### Safety & Trust
- **Reporting:** Report listings, sellers, or messages. Integrated with Facebook's broader reporting system.
- **Moderation:** Facebook's AI moderation for prohibited items, scams, and policy violations. Community reporting.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Real-identity policy (Facebook accounts tied to real names); Purchase Protection for eligible transactions; scam-detection algorithms.

#### Social Features
- **Favourites:** Save listings to a "Saved" collection.
- **Sharing:** Share listings to Facebook feed, groups, or via Messenger.
- **Following:** No dedicated Marketplace follow feature, but users can follow sellers' Facebook pages or profiles.

## 4. Key Takeaways for Trevo

### Common Patterns Across Platforms
1. **Free listing is the standard.** Every platform lets users list items at no cost. Charging to list would be a competitive disadvantage.
2. **In-app messaging is universal.** All platforms provide a built-in chat to keep communication on-platform and reduce fraud.
3. **Buyer protection drives trust.** Platforms with integrated payments all offer some form of buyer protection through escrow. This is increasingly expected by users.
4. **Promoted listings are the primary revenue lever.** Every platform offers paid visibility boosts — this is the most common monetisation strategy alongside transaction fees.
5. **Ratings and reviews are essential.** All platforms except OLX have robust rating systems. Reputation builds trust and drives repeat engagement.
6. **Favourites/saved items are universal.** Every platform allows bookmarking listings; several use this to trigger price-drop notifications, which re-engage buyers.

### Opportunities and Gaps
1. **Zero seller fees as a differentiator.** Vinted and Wallapop prove that a no-commission model attracts sellers. Trevo could adopt this approach and monetise through buyer fees and promoted listings instead.
2. **Negotiation UX matters.** Wallapop's one-tap offer and Vinted's in-chat offer button lower friction compared to platforms that rely on free-form chat. Trevo should build structured negotiation into the flow.
3. **Social features drive engagement.** Vinted benefits from follower feeds, likes, and community elements. A lightweight social layer (follow sellers, activity feed) could increase retention for Trevo.
4. **Local + shipped hybrid model.** Most platforms lean either local (OLX, Wallapop, FB Marketplace) or shipped (Vinted, eBay). Offering both modes with a seamless UX would broaden Trevo's appeal in the Portuguese market.
5. **Authenticity and quality assurance.** Only eBay has a formal authenticity programme. A lighter verification process (e.g., condition guarantee, photo verification) could differentiate Trevo in categories like electronics or branded fashion.
6. **Portuguese market specifics.** OLX dominates in Portugal with a classifieds model. There is room for a platform that combines OLX's local strength with Vinted's shipping, buyer protection, and social features — purpose-built for the Portuguese user.

### Recommended Priority Features for Trevo
- Free listings with no seller commission
- In-app messaging with structured price offers
- Integrated payments with escrow-based buyer protection
- 5-star rating and review system
- Favourites with price-drop alerts
- Promoted listings as a monetisation tool
- Support for both local meetup and shipped transactions
- Follow sellers and activity feed
